One village, two townships: who to ask about what

Maple Glen is a Census-designated place, not a borough, and it straddles two townships in Montgomery County. By Census boundary files, roughly half its area lies in Upper Dublin Township and half in Horsham Township. That split decides which office issues your permit and which authority serves your pipes.

Upper Dublin states that permits must be issued before work starts, and Code Enforcement takes calls at (215) 643-1600. Its sewer lateral guide calls for a Sewer Inspection Permit on all lateral work, plus a Highway Opening Permit if digging reaches the road. On the Horsham side, permit rules vary, so ask the Horsham Township building department.

Water follows the same split. Upper Dublin lists three suppliers (Aqua Pennsylvania, Ambler Borough Water Company and North Wales Water Authority) and calls its map a rough guide, so read your bill. In Horsham Township, the Horsham Water and Sewer Authority distributes water and runs the sewer system. For hardness, test your tap or ask your water utility.

Upper Dublin's lateral guide names two sewer providers that may apply, the Bucks County Water and Sewer Authority and the Upper Moreland-Hatboro Joint Sewer Authority. It does not say who owns which stretch of pipe, so ask your authority where its responsibility ends before you pay for a dig.

Burst pipe or active leak in Maple Glen: what to do while you wait

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Four steps limit the damage while a plumber is on the way.

Close the main shutoff

The main valve usually sits where the water line enters the basement, near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If water is surfacing in the yard or street, the break is on the utility side, so call your water supplier too.

Keep water away from electricity

If water reaches the panel, outlets or a sump pump cord, stay out of the room. If you smell gas near a water heater or boiler, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first.

Drain the lines

With the main closed, open the lowest faucet and one upstairs to relieve pressure at the break. Switch off the water heater's power or gas so the tank does not run dry.

Protect what you can

Lift rugs and electronics off wet floors and photograph the damage for your insurer. Then call (215) 714-6692 and tell dispatch which floor is wet and whether the main is off.

What affects the price of plumbing work in Maple Glen

The same symptom can be a quick fix or a day of work. These factors move the number.

Where the pipe is

A line in open basement joists takes less labor than one behind a finished ceiling or wall.

Pipe age and material

A 1982 house may have copper, plastic or polybutylene lines, and replacing a run differs from fixing one joint.

Permits and inspections

Upper Dublin requires a Sewer Inspection Permit for lateral work. Permit steps add time and fees that we flag before work starts.

Repair or replacement

A new pump switch is a small job. A battery backup or a tank swap with new venting is larger.

Night and weekend calls cost more than daytime bookings. If the leak has stopped, a same-day appointment when available may suit you.
