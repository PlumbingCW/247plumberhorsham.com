Butler Avenue, Rosemary Avenue and the blocks around them

Ambler's core runs along Butler Avenue, where the 1928 Ambler Theater stands among shops, with Victorian, Gothic and Colonial houses on the side streets. Borough Hall sits at 131 Rosemary Avenue, with its parking lot entered from Poplar Street. When you call, name the cross street and whether water comes from a wall, pipe or drain.

The Ambler Boiler House (built in 1897 by Keasbey & Mattison, now offices) and the factory building housing Ridge Hall show how much of the borough grew around mills. Buildings that old have usually been patched for generations, so we check what is in the wall before we price a swap.

Why Ambler's housing age drives the plumbing calls

Census estimates for 2020 to 2024 put the median Ambler home at 1954, with about 989 of 2,864 housing units dating from 1939 or earlier. Roughly two of every three predate 1960. These are estimates with margins of error.

Old sewer laterals

Pipe from that era is commonly clay tile or cast iron (general knowledge, not a borough finding). Roots enter joints, so a slow drain often means the lateral.

Galvanized and aged supply lines

Galvanized steel clogs from the inside and loses pressure before it leaks.

Stuck or missing shutoffs

Valves that have not turned in decades can seize or snap when forced.

The asbestos history, kept practical

EPA records that asbestos production was a vital part of Ambler's economy for about six decades from the late 19th century. That history concerns the factory sites, not your pipes, but old wrap on boiler and pipe runs in pre-1980 houses sometimes contains asbestos (general knowledge).

Do not scrape or cut old wrap. We stop the water, but we do not test for or remove asbestos.

Sewer laterals and the Ambler resale inspection

The Borough of Ambler runs its own sewer department, and its treatment plant discharges to the Wissahickon Creek. The borough's resale use and occupancy rules (Ordinance 1135) call for a master plumber's video inspection of the sanitary sewer service lateral before a sale, so sewer camera inspection is a routine request for sellers here. Confirm current requirements with the Code Department at 215-646-1000, extension 112.

Borough pages do not say where your responsibility for the lateral ends, so ask before anyone digs. If the camera shows a break or root mass, we scope sewer line repair and say whether cleaning will hold.

Water service lines, lead letters and hard water

The Ambler Borough Water Department draws from deep wells and one spring well. Its 2025 report states total hardness ranged from 252 to 380 mg/L as CaCO3, which leaves scale on heating elements and boilers. It also lists a 90th percentile lead result of 2.3 ppb against a 15 ppb action level.

The borough catalogued service lines on both sides of the meter and sends letters when a material is unknown or lead or galvanized is confirmed. If you got one, we handle the home side with water service line repair or repiping. Scale shortens tank life, so water heater repair often starts with a flush.

Boilers and radiators in pre-1960 homes

If your house is among the pre-1960 majority, it may heat with a boiler, though many have been converted. A cold radiator, a boiler that short-cycles or a puddle at its base calls for boiler repair, with same-day appointments when available.

Basement water near Tannery Run and the Wissahickon

The borough owns the storm sewer that carries runoff to Tannery Run, and it notes about four feet of precipitation a year, with runoff reaching the Wissahickon. The practical question is whether basement water is groundwater, a pipe or a backed-up drain.

Water after heavy rain points to seepage or a tired sump pump, while a pipe leak keeps running in dry weather. Sewage at a floor drain needs drain cleaning, and a split supply line is burst pipe repair.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

These steps fit older Ambler houses with stone or brick foundations.

Find the main in the basement

The main is usually where the service line enters the foundation wall, sometimes behind boxes or a panel. Turn it clockwise slowly and stop if it grinds.

Leave the curb valve alone

The street or sidewalk valve is usually the utility's. If the main will not close, call the borough water department and us.

Drain the line and cut the power risk

Open the lowest cold tap to relieve pressure. If water reaches the panel, do not step in it.

Lift, photograph and keep drains closed

Lift boxes off the floor and photograph the damage. With sewage, avoid contact and run no water.
