Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Written for Warrington basements, garages and crawl spaces.

Shut off the main

Find where the line enters the house, often a basement or utility room, and turn the valve clockwise. If it will not turn, do not force it. Tell us when you call.

Cut power near standing water

If water reaches outlets or the panel, stay out and shut breakers only from a dry spot. Never wade into a flooded basement with live circuits.

Drain and protect

Open the lowest faucet to relieve pressure, lift boxes off the floor, and photograph the damage for your insurance carrier.

Keep clear of sewage

If a drain is backing up, stop running water, keep children and pets away, and skip drain chemicals, which can harm the pipe and the technician.
